## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [synapse-architect.com](https://synapse-architect.com) using email, Google, GitHub, or Salesforce OAuth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Salesforce org">
    Go to **Settings → Connections** and authorize your Salesforce organization. Synapse uses OAuth 2.0 — no credentials are stored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Create a project and link it to your Salesforce org and GitHub repository. Projects are the workspace where specialists run and deliver code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first Blueprint">
    Describe a change in natural language, let Synapse compose a Blueprint, and execute it with one click. Specialists push results to a Git branch for your review.
  </Step>
</Steps>
